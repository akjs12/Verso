Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026. This is a plain-language summary of how Verso handles your data on this site.

1. Information we collect

When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect the email address you provide. When you submit the contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message. We do not require an account to read articles, like posts, or save bookmarks — those preferences are stored locally in your browser instead.

2. How we use it

We use your email address only to send the newsletter you signed up for, or to reply to a message you sent us. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

3. Cookies & local storage

This site uses your browser's localStorage to remember your theme preference, liked posts, and bookmarked articles. This information stays on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. Clearing your browser data will reset these preferences.

4. Third-party services

Images on this demo site are served from a third-party placeholder image provider for illustrative purposes. In a production deployment, any analytics or embed providers would be listed here along with links to their own privacy policies.

5. Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in every email we send. You can clear your bookmarks and likes at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.

We built Verso to be a calm place to read. That philosophy extends to how we handle your data: collect the minimum, explain it plainly, and never sell it.

6. Children's privacy

Verso is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, note the change in our newsletter.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page, or directly to ramjadhav9592@gmail.com.
