Our story

Writing for readers, not algorithms

Verso started as a shared notes document between three friends who missed reading things that took longer than ninety seconds. It's grown into a small editorial team — still writing for the same reason.

Why we exist

Our mission

Most of the internet is optimized to keep you scrolling. We're optimizing for the opposite: articles you finish, ideas you remember, and a reading experience calm enough that you'd actually want to come back to it tomorrow.

We publish two to three long-form pieces a week across eight beats — technology, design, productivity, culture, science, travel, business, and wellness — each one reported, drafted, and edited by a small team who actually specializes in that subject.

What we believe

Our values

🕰️

Depth over speed

We'd rather publish a well-reported piece next week than a rushed one today.

🎯

Specific over vague

Concrete detail beats a confident-sounding generalization, every time.

🪟

Transparent by default

Corrections are dated and visible. We don't quietly edit the record.

🧭

Independent

No investors telling us what to cover, and no ads shaping the coverage.

🌱

Sustainably paced

Two to three articles a week, forever, beats twenty a week for six months.

🔎

Curious first

We chase the question we can't stop thinking about, not the trending topic.
